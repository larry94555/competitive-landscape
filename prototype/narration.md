# Demo narration

**This file is the script.** Edit the text under each heading, then run:

```
python prototype/build.py
```

That regenerates the subtitles and the demo page. **No re-recording is needed** — the video
holds no text of its own. Re-record only when what happens *on screen* changes.

Rules for the writing are in
[../docs/Video_Text_Best_Practices.md](../docs/Video_Text_Best_Practices.md) (what the words
say) and [../docs/Video_Guidelines.md](../docs/Video_Guidelines.md) (how the film is made).
The short version: every line is a mini elevator pitch — one idea, 10 to 18 words, stating a
fact whose point the viewer works out unaided. Never sell, never explain the plumbing, and
never tell someone something is easy.

- The `[id]` in each heading ties the line to a moment in the demo. **Do not change it.**
- The time is when the line appears, and the gap to the next one is how long it is on screen.
- Leave a line **empty** to keep the previous caption up through that moment.
- `~Ns` is roughly how long the line takes to read aloud. If it is longer than the gap, that is
  fine when narration is on (the video waits), but the caption will feel rushed when it is off.
